# Landing page biru muda

Referensi susunan: https://maukuliah.id/, dilihat 8 Oktober 2026.
Implementasi: Blade dan CSS, tema light dengan biru muda, route dan navigasi lama dipertahankan.

Aset dibuat memakai built-in imagegen, kemudian dioptimalkan ke WebP. Enam fitur berasal dari satu sprite sheet 3 x 2. Foto dan logo kampus berasal dari aset proyek.

## Prompt hero

Friendly anime-inspired educational illustration of two Indonesian high school students, boy and girl smiling and waving, white school shirts, sky-blue ties, blue-gray uniform, floating books and graduation cap. Clean outlines, pastel shading, no text, no logos, transparent background.

## Prompt fitur

Six equal scenes in a 3-column by 2-row sprite sheet, pastel sky-blue editorial illustration: girl discovering interests; university building with students; student studying subjects; online exam with laptop and clock; graduation with scholarship certificate and coins; professionals discussing career. No text or logos.

## Logo Siapkuliah

Built-in imagegen: simple open-book symbol forming M, suggesting graduation and forward ambition; two flat colors azure and sky blue, rounded geometry, no text, transparent background. Wordmark Siapkuliah rendered as live text in the navbar and footer.
